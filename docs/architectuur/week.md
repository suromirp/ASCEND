**Dragging a training in Week** (`engine/weekDrag.ts`, `components/useWeekDrag.ts`, `components/useVerticalDrag.ts`, decision
`docs/beslissingen/2026-10-28-slepen-in-week.md`, design `docs/visie/Slepen in Week.md`): the handle in the row (only the handle, no long
press) moves a training to another day of the shown week, never to another week. It is a faster hand, not a new rule: letting go goes
through `Week.tsx#moveTo` and `decideMove`, the same road as "Verplaatsen" in the action sheet (same lock, `movedFromDate` and
`ChangeNotice` with Ongedaan maken). Clean is applied at once; a cascade, an unresolvable clash, your rest day, a day the time does not
fit and any test open `RescheduleDialog` with Akkoord. Logged, past, skipped, rest, race and holiday or trek trainings have no handle
(`dragEligibility`); the verdict per day (`dayVerdicts`, built on `keptDayReason` from `moveSuggestions.ts`) is mint when it fits, amber
when it asks, muted when it cannot. The pointer mechanics are shared with the goals on Ascend (`useVerticalDrag`, `edgeScroll`); a change
there changes both. Setting "Trainingen slepen in Week" (`dragWeek`, absent = on).

**Ruilen in Week** (`engine/weekSwap.ts`, `storage/weekSwap.ts`, decision `docs/beslissingen/2026-11-02-trainingen-wisselen.md`): two trainings of the shown week trade days, through "Ruilen" in the action sheet (always, the list is `swapOptions`) or by letting go while dragging on a day with exactly one training that may move (`withSwapVerdicts` turns that day into kind `swap`, mint when clean and amber when it asks; with two or more, or one that may not move, it stays a plain move). Who may swap is who may drag (`dragEligibility`), both in the same week, never across two. `checkSwap` checks both directions on the week after the swap: `findHeavyConflicts` (48 hours, long run, fixed sports, a test), the rest day and the closed days (`keptDayReason`), the time (`dayHasRoomFor`); `decideSwap` adds `legsCloseAfterSwap`. Clean is applied at once with "A en B geruild" and Ongedaan maken; otherwise `RescheduleDialog` (eyebrow Ruilen, the dry reason) built like a move: a clash, your rest day, too little time or two heavy leg days in a row have Liever niet as the main button and "Toch ruilen" second; only a test or a training that moved for a holiday asks plain Akkoord (decision `docs/beslissingen/2026-11-02-ruilen-zoals-verplaatsen.md`). Two trainings together on a day that do not combine well get the quiet "Beter niet op één dag". `applyProposal` writes a proposal with `swap: true` through `writeSwap`: one transaction, both or neither, and nothing when one of the two moved or was logged in the meantime; a failure is said in `ChangeNotice` without undo (`showFailure`, `Week.tsx#applyOrTell`). Undo goes through `undoSwap`: only when both rows are still exactly what the swap wrote, else a notice that it can no longer be undone. Setting "Loslaten op een training" (`dropOnTraining`, Ruilen as advice, Samen; in the sheet "Trainingen slepen in Week") decides what letting go while dragging does; the action sheet always offers Ruilen. A scan (`components/silentCatch.scan.test.ts`) refuses a `.catch` in pages or components that only logs. Both rows get your lock and `movedFromDate` like a move (back on its own day it is the plan's again). The planning run and `planGolden.test.ts` do not move.

**Inhalen** (`engine/catchUp.ts`, `storage/catchUpApply.ts`, decision `docs/beslissingen/2026-10-29-gemiste-training-inhalen.md`): a missed
training (past, no log, not skipped, no test, race, rest walk or leave, this or last week) can be caught up through the same road as
moving: "Inhalen" in the action sheet and the handle in Week (setting "Trainingen slepen in Week"), only to today or later. The old row is
not moved: `applyProposal` skips it (your lock) and adds a new row with the same template and length on the chosen day (your lock,
`PlannedSession.catchUp`); undo puts the old one back and removes the new one. A clash still asks Akkoord. Rows say "ingehaald van wo 28"
and "ingehaald op do 29". The automatic planning does not touch this, `planGolden.test.ts` does not move.

**De dag van een log in Week** (`engine/logDay.ts`, decision `docs/beslissingen/2026-10-29-dag-van-een-training.md`): to show, a MacroFactor log counts on the day of its planned training when that is exactly the day after `completedDate` (begun before midnight, finished after), so the row and the chart "Belasting per dag" say the same; the log is never rewritten. Only display uses it (Week, the weekly review, Vandaag, the Logboek calendar); leg index, load over 7 and 28 days, readiness and planning stay on `completedDate`.

**De rustdag terug** (`engine/restDayBack.ts`, decision `docs/beslissingen/2026-11-01-rustdag-terug.md`): an empty day on your fixed rest day reads "Rustdag · je vaste rustdag" with the hammock, display only. When a proposal let the Herstel row of that day go (skipped with the lock of a proposal, never yours, never a leave mark) and the day is empty again, rule REST-BACK gives a proposal with Akkoord that brings the same row back without a lock; it shows on Vandaag and in the Rustdag card of that week. After Akkoord the row stays in `removedByPlanIds` (`state/adviceAndWeek.ts`, built from every accepted `remove`): it is planned again, but a later rule that reads that set must check the status of the row, not only its id. Setting "Rustdag terugzetten voorstellen" (`restDayBack`, absent = on). The planning run and `planGolden.test.ts` do not move.

**Rustdag deze week** (`engine/restDayThisWeek.ts`, shown through `emptyDayText` in `engine/restDayBack.ts`): when a training is planned or logged on your fixed rest day (a testloop on Monday) and the week has no Herstel row or holiday day, the first empty free day of the week (`freeDaysOfWeek`, no row, no log, no band), preferably not right before a heavy day (leg heavy, hard run, long run; a missed one in the past does not count), reads "Rustdag deze week · je rustdag op maandag is bezet" with the hammock instead of "Vrij". Display only: nothing moves and nothing is stored. With no free day left the existing REST-DAY concern and its proposal with Akkoord speak. **Kaarten rond de missie op Vandaag** (`engine/todayCards.ts`, `pages/Today.tsx`, decision `docs/beslissingen/2026-11-01-vandaag-volgorde.md`): the cards that compete with the mission for the top of Vandaag have one fixed rank: reported ill (`ill`), the early illness sign, the morning card, building back after illness (`ramp`), the advice card (`CoachCard`, also the rest day back), the missed training card, Nieuw begonnen, the backup reminder. `arrangeToday` puts at most the chosen number above the mission (setting "Kaarten boven de missie", `cardsAboveMission`, 1 as advice, 2 or `all`; absent = 1) and the rest directly under it in the same order. `ill` and the illness sign are safety: always above, even past the maximum, and they count toward it. `ramp` asks nothing, so the cards that ask an answer go first in the days of building back. Advice and the missed training card never stand above the mission. Today renders two keyed lists with the mission between them, so the mission itself never moves when a card appears or goes (measured: its breathing keeps running and no animation starts); a card that crosses from below to above is mounted again, which shows nothing because these cards have no entry animation. Whether a card is there comes from its own hook (`useIllnessSign`, `useFreshStart`, `useMissedTraining`), not from the card, so Vandaag knows before it renders. A new card that wants the top of Vandaag gets a place in `TODAY_CARD_RANK`, never a spot of its own above the mission. The memory of the missed training card lives in `AppDataContext` (`missedMemory`, `rememberMissed` with a sequence number so an older read never overwrites a newer answer).

**Week opnieuw plannen** (`engine/replanWeek.ts`, `storage/weekReplan.ts`, `state/weekReplanActions.ts`, `components/WeekReplanButton.tsx`, decision `docs/beslissingen/2026-11-02-week-opnieuw-plannen.md`): a quiet button under the week, only for the shown week when it still has today or later. `replanWeek` lays the week pattern of that week (`patternSessions`, all seven days) over what stays: logged or past rows, fixed sports, a race, rows a holiday or tocht marked (`leave`), what you skipped yourself, and rows on an illness or build-back day (`illnessHeld`). What stays takes its slot in the pattern (same template, nearest day; a test the nearest `hard_run` slot). Open slots reuse a free row of the same template (planned first, then one a proposal skipped, so that comes back), stripped of lock, `movedFromDate`, `plannedMinutes` and proposal marks; a row whose slot is already past keeps its day without a lock; the rest goes. Then the same pass as the sync (`previewProgramHorizon` with `releaseAnchors: false`, which now also returns `remaining`); a test that stays moves only to a day where the week has fewer remaining findings (`betterTestDay`). `weekDiff` gives what changes, a new row of a template that goes keeps that row's id. Only the shown week and the week after it are written (what the pass does across the week's edge, so the sync afterwards repairs nothing; the week after counts in the test's score too and the proposal says so): `writeWeekReplan` in one transaction, together with the accepted proposal in `PlanChangeProposalsRepo`, nothing when a row of those two weeks changed in the meantime (`REPLAN_STALE`), when a row to write was not among them at planning time, or when a write fails halfway. A training open in Forge stays (`keepIds`); a caught-up training that goes back is named. `ProposalSheet` shows it with Liever niet and Akkoord and the sentence "Ook wat je zelf verplaatste gaat terug naar het plan."; without a difference it says "Je week staat al zoals het plan hem maakt." with only Begrepen (`answerless`). A swap of a test brings nothing back. The planning run and `planGolden.test.ts` do not move.
